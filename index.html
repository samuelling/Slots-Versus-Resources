Pinball Party

Your whole phone is a pinball table: tap the left and right halves to flip. Shoot the ball out the top and it flies into the NULL ZONE, where everyone controls something weird. What happens to it there decides how hard it hits the next table.

On iPhone, tap Allow when asked about motion: you'll need it when you get the tilt controls.
